      About this dashboard

      Project

      Vox studies what happens when M2D, a general audio model pre-trained on AudioSet, is further pre-trained on a small, open music collection with one GPU, and how the result compares with music-specific models.

      Method

      M2D is further pre-trained on 10,000 tracks of the Free Music Archive with background noise, a frozen copy of the original model as a teacher, and a pitch (CQT) teacher. Its frozen features are then evaluated with linear probes.

      How the demo works

      Your clip is sent to the server, converted to 16 kHz mono, and its first 6.08 s are turned into a log-mel spectrogram with M2D’s own front-end. The frozen encoder produces a 3,840-value embedding, and a linear probe trained on the task’s dataset turns it into scores. Every number shown comes from the model; nothing is pre-written.

      Models

      The original M2D (M2D/0.7, NTT) and the further pre-trained M2D. The Results tab also lists public music models (MERT, MusicFM, MATPAC++) for reference, by their scores only.

      Datasets

      GTZAN (genre), MagnaTagATune (tagging), NSynth (instrument and pitch) and FMA-small (genre, reported separately). No GTZAN audio is bundled.
